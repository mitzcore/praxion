# Praxion demo video — storyboard for VCs and technical founders

Target length 2:30–3:00. Two audiences in one cut: the first 90 seconds need no engineering background; the last 60 seconds add the technical proof. Every number and every curve comes from one real recorded run, `artifacts/runs/v13-s11-20261031-020922-a0e68e` (Meta `muse-spark-1.3`, protocol v1.3), plus the five-machine benchmark in `docs/BENCHMARKS.md`. Nothing is illustrative.

Visual language: dark background, one accent for "water level", amber for "before", green for "after", red line for "overflow". Generated motion (Higgsfield) is used only for the physical metaphor shots; all data shots are the real charts and dashboard frames in `artifacts/video/assets/`.

- Beats 2 and 5 use real replay footage from `dashboard_replay.mp4` (segment offsets in `artifacts/demo/v13-s11-20261031-020922-a0e68e/segments.json`).
- Beat 1 is the only fully generated shot (Higgsfield): a clean industrial water tank with a pump inlet and a drain, slow camera push, no text in the generated frame; captions are overlaid in edit.
